Privacy policy

Clubstride Pro (Android · package com.clubstride.mobile)
Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Operator: Clubstride · Contact: support@clubstride.com

Clubstride Pro is a coaching app for football clubs. This page explains what the app stores, why, and how to remove it. It describes the app as it is today.

Who is responsible

Clubstride (“we”) operates Clubstride Pro. For any question about this policy or your data, email support@clubstride.com.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, plus your first and last name if you enter them.
  • Club records you create: team names, player names, shirt numbers and positions, training days, attendance marks (present, late, absent, excused) and the time each mark was recorded.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, files, health data, advertising identifiers, analytics, or crash reports. The app contains no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and show you the squads you belong to (performance of our agreement with you).
  • To store and sync the attendance register across the coaches who mark it (same basis).
  • To keep the service secure and working, for example preventing one club from reading another club’s records (our legitimate interest).

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database operated by our hosting provider, Supabase, with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encryption at rest. Access is restricted by per-team database policies: staff of a team see that team, and a parent or guardian sees only their own child.

Who can see it

Only the people your club has added to the team. We do not sell data, do not share it for advertising, and do not disclose it except where the law requires it or where a hosting provider processes it on our behalf under contract.

Children

The app is for adult coaches and club staff — it is not directed at children. The squad records it stores are about players, who may be minors, and are entered by the coach. The club decides what it records and is responsible for having the basis to record it.

How long we keep it

Account and club records are kept while the account or team exists. When an account is deleted, the login, email, name, memberships and guardian links are removed. Records that belong to the club — squads, players, attendance, feedback with the author detached — stay with the team, because they are the club’s register, not the individual’s. Backups roll off on the host’s normal backup cycle.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. On the web: delete-account. A coach who is the only manager of a team must hand the team over first; the app explains this when you try.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correction of it, or its deletion, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Email support@clubstride.com. If you are in the EU/EEA you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Local storage

The mobile app keeps your session and any unsent attendance marks on the device so it works without signal. Clearing app data or deleting your account removes them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update this page and the date above.
