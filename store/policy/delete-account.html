Delete your Clubstride Pro account

Applies to Clubstride Pro for Android (com.clubstride.mobile). Contact: support@clubstride.com

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Clubstride Pro and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

The app tells you first how many changes are still only on the phone, so nothing you recorded offline is lost by surprise.

Delete it by email

If you cannot sign in, email support@clubstride.com from the address on the account and ask for deletion. We may ask you to confirm you control the account. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login, email address and name.
  • Your team memberships and guardian links.
  • Push/device tokens registered to the account.
  • Any unsent attendance marks held on the device are discarded with the session.

What stays

Records that belong to the club rather than to you: teams, players, fixtures, attendance marks, drills and coaching feedback. Where you wrote those records, your name is detached from them. This is deliberate — deleting a coach should not delete a club’s register or another child’s history.

If you manage a team

A sole manager cannot delete their account until another manager is appointed, otherwise the team and its players would be left with nobody able to administer them. The app names the teams involved; hand them over in Settings first, then delete.
