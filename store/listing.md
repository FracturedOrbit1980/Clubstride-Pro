## Full description (4000)

Clubstride Pro is the coach's side of Clubstride: the squad list, the week's
training days, and the attendance register — on your phone, at the pitch, with
or without signal.

Built for match-day reality
• Works offline. Marks recorded with no signal are queued on the phone and sync
  when the connection returns; nothing is silently dropped.
• Attendance in two taps. Present, late, absent or excused for every player,
  for every session of the week.
• Register as a PDF. Export the week as an A4 attendance register — squad by
  session, with totals, percentages and per-session summaries — ready to print
  or share.
• One week, one screen. Move between weeks to review or correct earlier
  sessions.
• Roles that match a club. Coaches and managers run their own squads; parents
  see only their own child.

Made for coaches, not administrators
Setup takes a minute: add your squad, tick the days you train, and start
marking. No spreadsheets, no clipboards blowing across the pitch.

About your data
Attendance and squad details are stored in your club's account and protected
per team. Accounts can be deleted from inside the app (Settings → Delete
account). Clubstride Pro shows no ads and sells no data.

Clubstride Pro is a companion to Clubstride Lite, the offline match-day coach
app.
